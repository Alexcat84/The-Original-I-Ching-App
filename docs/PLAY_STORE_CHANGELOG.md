## 4.1.8 (versionCode 58)

### English (en-US)

```text
Fidelity and transparency update.
- Oracle texts audited against the original editions: Wilhelm/Baynes (1950), James Legge (Sacred Books of the East XVI, 1882) and the Zhou Yi in classical Chinese (Chinese Text Project).

- New public Fidelity Audits page and a clearer FAQ on changing lines.
```
